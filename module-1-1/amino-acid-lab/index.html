Fischer projections: L or D?

Objective 2. Determine whether an amino acid Fischer projection is L or D.

In a Fischer projection, horizontal bonds come out toward you ("reaching out for a hug") and vertical bonds go back. Use CORN: with H pointing toward you, if COO⁻ → R → NH₃⁺ runs clockwise, it's L. If H points away, flip the answer.

Titration curves

Objective 4. Interpret titration curves for amino acids, polypeptides and other polyprotic species.

Read the curve

We start fully protonated at low pH and add strong base. Each flat region is one group being titrated, and its midpoint (0.5, 1.5, 2.5 … equivalents) gives that group's pKa. Net charge falls by one with each equivalent. The pI is the equivalence point where net charge reaches 0.
